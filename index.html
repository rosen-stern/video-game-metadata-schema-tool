Video Game Metadata Schema Tool

About
Visualizer

Welcome to the Video Game Metadata Schema (VGMS) tool! Here you can interact with the VGMS terms in visualized graphs.

The VGMS was created by: Lee, J. H., Schmalz, M., Newman, M., & Koughan, L. D. Last updated in 2024. Retrieved from: https://github.com/uwgamergroup/video-game-metadata-schema

Designed by nina navazio for LIS 536: Metadata for Interactive Media at the University of Washington.

PRs and contributions welcome! :)
